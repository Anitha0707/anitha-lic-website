              Frequently Asked Questions

              What is the best LIC plan for a salaried person?+
              Jeevan Labh (936) and New Jeevan Anand (915) are excellent — limited premium, strong returns, and life cover. Pair with Digi Term (876) for maximum protection at the lowest cost.
              How do I start a child plan with LIC?+
              Jeevan Tarun (934) and Amritbaal (874) are ideal. You can start from birth — premiums are low and the policy continues even if something happens to the parent.
              What is the difference between term and endowment?+
              Term gives pure life protection at very low premiums — no maturity payout. Endowment combines savings + life cover so you receive a lump sum at maturity regardless.
              Can I get a pension plan if I am self-employed?+
              Absolutely. Jeevan Akshay VII (857) and New Jeevan Shanti (858) are perfect — with no employer PF, your own pension plan is essential.
              How does LIC claim settlement work?+
              LIC has one of India's highest claim settlement ratios. Claims can be filed online via licindia.in or through your advisor. Anitha personally guides all her clients through the full claim process.
